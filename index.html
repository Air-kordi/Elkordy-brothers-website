Origin

The first team ever assembled.

On May 1, 2024, Elkordy Brothers Basketball was assembled for its first official game against Barangay Sarapan Basketball Team at the Brgy. Sarapan Gym in Passi City. Barangay Sarapan entered the matchup as champions of the Inter-Barangay tournament held at the Passi City Arena.

Coach Mohamed Mogib Elkordy assembled the team with Zaldy Patizar and Rolen Pantin leading the squad. The starting five featured Addy Villanueva, John Sampollo, Rolen Pantin, RJ Gonzaga, and Zaldy Patizar. Completing the roster were Emmanuel Pet, Francis Vergara Arriesgado, Sean Espinosa, John Galvan, Rhey Orilla, Benedict Masculino, and Cyrus Tuya.

Elkordy Brothers took the win, 87–83 — the opening chapter of the brotherhood’s legacy.

Head Coach · Est. 2024

Mohamed Mogib
Elkordy

The architect of Elkordy Brothers Basketball. Coach Mohamed Mogib Elkordy founded and assembled the team for its first official game on May 1, 2024 — a hard-fought victory over Barangay Sarapan that marked the beginning of the brotherhood’s competitive journey.

Built on discipline, loyalty, and a shared love for the game, his leadership continues to define the culture carried into every tournament run and community initiative.
